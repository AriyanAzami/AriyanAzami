### 🧑‍💻 About me

I'm a Computer Engineering student at York University who likes building software that ships and models that work. Currently a Software Developer at [Appli Solutions](https://applisolutions.com), an AI-powered hiring platform, and a Machine Learning Research Assistant at York's Laboratory of Advanced Biotechnologies, where I work on deep learning for medical imaging. My path into engineering started early — with competitive robotics back in junior high — and hasn't slowed down since.

---

### 🔭 What I'm working on right now

- 💻 **Production software at Appli Solutions** — shipping backend and frontend features across the full stack of an AI-powered hiring platform.
- 🧠 **Medical imaging research** — training U-Net segmentation models and exploring Stable Diffusion–based generative pipelines for diagnostic imaging at York's Laboratory of Advanced Biotechnologies.
- 🤖 **Computer vision for autonomous systems** — co-developed a real-time obstacle-detection dataset and YOLO model, released open-source on [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/abtinzandi/obstacle-detection-dataset) and [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Abtinz/Obstacle-Detection-Dataset-YOLO).
